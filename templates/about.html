Method & limits

What an inspection tells you.

EfficientNet feature fusion

The active model combines 1,280 EfficientNet-B0 features, 63 DCT coefficients and 63 pHash bits in a trained MLP. The backbone was fine-tuned for facial manipulation on FF++ C23, then frozen for the feature-fusion comparison. Each receipt records the exported model hash and training manifest hash.

Video processing

Every video frame is decoded. Guarded pHash similarity can reuse a nearby anchor score, with a forced refresh at least every 12 frames. Reused frames retain their temporal weight. Approximate reuse can miss subtle or brief edits, and a clip with reused frames cannot receive a low-signal verdict. Uploads are limited to 3,600 frames and 120 seconds.

Evaluation and limits

The benchmark compares the trained EfficientNet baseline on every frame, a deep-feature MLP, fusion on every frame, and fusion with pHash reuse. Source-pair splits do not prove identity-disjoint generalization. The model screens FF++ facial manipulations; fully AI-generated video detection is not included. Audio, lip synchronization, pulse signals and face localization are not analyzed.

Scores are uncalibrated screening signals. Treat every result as a reason for human review, not a claim about authenticity.

Receipts and blockchain

Receipts are signed with a server-held HMAC key and stored in SQLite. Verification checks receipt integrity and exact file bytes. When configured, a browser wallet can publish receipt or benchmark hashes to the publisher-restricted Ethereum registry. The server checks the chain, sender, transaction calldata and event before saving an anchor link. A hash or blockchain record cannot establish whether depicted content is true. Deployment instructions and the contract are in the repository.
